# Last Animal — v0.5.0 (2026-10-27)

Second GA of the full game on both platforms. Everything below is what
changed for a player since v0.4.3 (2026-10-19).

## What shipped

- **Isometric view is now the default.** Launching the game gives you the
  fixed isometric camera (owner ruling for the release: "Iso,"). If you want
  the old follow camera, launch with `--classic-cam`.
- **Day/night mood lighting.** The world's light follows the in-game clock:
  dusk warms, night dims. The canyon horizon now dims to the night sky
  instead of a bright rectangle floating over it.
- **Death means death.** At Life 0 you go down and stay down — no attacking,
  interacting, travelling or casting as a corpse, and no Manna banking.
  Reviving is loading your last save (`F9`) or starting fresh.
- **No spawn ambush.** The goblin camp in the opening meadow is fielded
  outside the aggro ring: a fresh boot no longer loses most of its Life in
  the first ~15 seconds before you can act.
- **Quest line fixed.** Quest beats now advance in order regardless of the
  order you kill bosses in, and loading a save no longer rewinds a beat you
  already completed.
- **HUD windows never overlap.** Dialogue and the Empathy Book no longer
  print over the Life/Manna gauge block — each surface has its own band.
- **Wild recruits are real, and refusals say why.** The wild creature in the
  boot zone can actually join now: calm it (`F`), take the offer (`E`), pay
  the first wage (`P`). A skill press that is refused (for example Mend
  short of Manna) now prints the reason instead of failing silently.
- **Ground-plate gate.** A mis-pathed ground texture can no longer pass the
  release checks — the "white ground" class of shipping bug is gated.

## Known limits (stated, not hidden)

- **Art is directional, not final.** Characters, tiles and sky in this
  build are the agreed art *direction* — not a finished quality bar. The
  D2R-grade look promised as the milestone's ambition is NOT shipped in
  v0.5.0; it stays open.
- **Icons ruling is open.** The generated icon set has no product consumer
  yet — no icons shipped into the game in this release.
- **Engine shutdown noise.** On exit, the engine can print known "resource
  in use" noise lines (the named resource-in-use class). They affect
  nothing while you play; stated here instead of hidden.
- **One crash observed, not reproduced.** A single mid-play crash was seen
  once during a full playthrough and has not reproduced since. If you hit
  one, note what you were doing and report the exact text.
- **Windows evidence is the export gate + owner playtests.** The Windows
  binary passes the PE + embedded-assembly export gates and was played on
  Windows by the owner; on the Linux build host there is no working wine,
  so no automated Windows render smoke exists — stated, not faked.

## Playtest tips for what's new

- Death: press `F9` to reload your last save — that IS the revive.
- Classic camera: run `LastAnimal.exe --classic-cam` (Windows) or
  `./LastAnimal.x86_64 --classic-cam` (Linux).
- Recruit: `F` on the meadow wild → `E` on the offer → `P` for its wage.
