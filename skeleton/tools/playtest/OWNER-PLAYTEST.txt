LAST ANIMAL - OWNER PLAYTEST (one page, MC 11129)

You are trying BOTH camera styles so you can pick the default.

WHAT TO DO
  1. Keep all files in THIS folder (the game LastAnimal.exe, the data_
     folder, the two .bat files). Do not move them apart.
  2. Play one round each way:
     - DOUBLE-CLICK  LAST-ANIMAL-CLASSIC.bat   = the CLASSIC camera
       (follows behind you, eases and lags a little).
     - DOUBLE-CLICK  LAST-ANIMAL-ISO.bat       = the ISO camera
       (a fixed isometric view - it never lags or swings, like a
       strategy game).
  3. While you play, the game writes its boot line to a log file in this
     folder (BOOT-LOG-CLASSIC.txt or BOOT-LOG-ISO.txt). When you CLOSE the
     game, the black window stays open and shows that line, e.g.
        LA_PLAYTEST_RIG=ISO (fixed isometric camera)
     That line is the proof of which camera you were actually looking at.
  4. Press any key in the black window to close it.

TELLING THE TWO APART WHILE YOU PLAY
  CLASSIC: walk around - the camera turns with you and eases into place
           behind you (smooth lag).
  ISO:     walk around - the view stays at the same fixed angle the whole
           time. It never swings.

IF NOTHING OPENS
  A window that flashes and vanishes in under a second usually means
  Windows blocked the download: right-click LAST-ANIMAL-CLASSIC.bat ->
  Properties -> check "Unblock" -> OK, then try again. The folder also
  gets a BOOT-LOG-*-err.txt file - if it says something about ".NET" or
  "hostfxr", the free "Microsoft .NET Desktop Runtime 8.0 (x64)" needs to
  be installed once (say so and we will do it for you).

WHAT YOUR PICK MEANS
  Playing both and naming your favourite sets the game's default camera.
  The choice is yours; nothing is saved about the camera and either
  launcher loads your save.
